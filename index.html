Our Story

A home where you can exhale

Hannah's House was created for women who deserve more than four walls — they deserve a foundation. Whether you're working, going to school, in recovery, or simply starting fresh, this is a space built with warmth, structure, and dignity.

Located in Minneapolis's Hawthorne neighborhood, every resident is screened and welcomed into an accountable, peaceful community. You bring your story. We provide the home.
